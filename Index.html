Chicago Riots: Blood on the Black Belt

The Black Belt

Chicago’s historic Black Belt was a narrow corridor on the South Side where Black residents were forced to live due to racist housing laws and violent intimidation. Despite hardship, it became a center of culture, activism, and community strength.

Hover for Quick Facts

1919 Beach Incident
A Black teenager, Eugene Williams, drifted into a whites-only area at the beach and was killed — triggering the Chicago Race Riot.
Segregation Pressure
Restrictive covenants and violent mobs kept Black families confined to overcrowded blocks in the Black Belt.
National Guard Deployment
During the 1968 unrest, thousands of troops patrolled Chicago streets after Dr. King’s assassination.
